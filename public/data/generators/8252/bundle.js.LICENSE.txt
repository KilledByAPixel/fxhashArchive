de|growth:generations

Copyright 2021 mrkswcz

Materials tend to take particular forms due to their physical properties,
technological necessities or natural limitations. However, what rules
should a simulated environment follow when no physical constraints are
inherent to the digital environment?

This project continues the exploration of digital forms that I began
on hicetnunc in 2021. Each token creates an image of a unique sculpture
generated with a custom growth/degrowth algorithm.

The image is rendered with a tailored raytracing engine. Depending on
the machine and the CPU usage, it may take a few seconds (on fast computers)
to up to a minute on mid-tier phones for the 800x800px image.

Click on the image to open the menu.
You can choose the resolution from preview quality to a print of approx.
30x30cm. You have the option to save the rendered image in PNG format.
(Live mode only. There may be minor differences in tone or contrast for
different resolutions.)

Click STL to download the digital model to view on your computer or 
3d print at home (experimental, live mode only).
